5. Cookies and similar technologies

Under Section 155(4) of the Electronic Communications Act and Article 5(3) of Directive 2002/58/EC, information may only be stored on, or read from, your device with your prior informed consent — except where it is strictly necessary for providing the service you explicitly requested.

This website stores the following:

WhatTypePurposeConsent requiredDuration
langlocalStorage entry, not a cookieRemembers the interface language you selectedNo — strictly necessary for a function you requestedUntil you clear your browser storage

That is the complete list. No cookies are set by this website, which is why you are not shown a cookie banner — there is nothing to consent to.

This is a deliberate design decision, not an oversight. Our forms are protected against automated abuse server-side — by a decoy field, a minimum completion time, and an origin check, all evaluated on our own systems — rather than by a third-party CAPTCHA service. A CAPTCHA widget would have set cookies, required your prior consent, and sent your device data to another company. It was rejected for those reasons and because it would not, in fact, have protected the endpoint that matters.

We use no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking or profiling cookies of any kind. We do not build behavioural profiles, we do not carry out automated decision-making within the meaning of GDPR Article 22, and we do not sell data to anybody, in any circumstances.

Where a transfer to a third country nevertheless takes place, it is carried out only on the basis of a guarantee recognised by Chapter V of the GDPR — an adequacy decision of the European Commission, standard contractual clauses, or another lawful mechanism — and we will identify it here. 8. Where we act as a processor

Where we host and operate a CRM or ERP system for a client, the personal data stored in that system is processed on the client's instructions. In that relationship:

  • the client is the controller and determines the purposes and means of processing;
  • we are the processor, and we act only on documented instructions;
  • the relationship is governed by a written data processing agreement meeting the requirements of GDPR Article 28;
  • we do not use that data for our own purposes, and we do not access it except as necessary for operation, support or a fault the client has reported;
  • if a data subject contacts us directly about data held in a client's system, we forward the request to the client without delay and assist them in answering it;
  • on termination, we return or delete the data at the client's choice, in accordance with the agreement.

9. Your rights

Your right to object — presented separately, as GDPR Article 21(4) requires

Where we process your data on the basis of our legitimate interests — sections 4.2, 4.3 and 4.6 — you may object at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation. We then stop processing unless we can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds which override your interests, rights and freedoms, or unless the processing is needed for legal claims.

If you object to direct marketing, we stop immediately and without exception.

Write to PRIVACY_EMAIL.

You have the following rights in relation to your personal data.

  • Right of access (Article 15) — to be told whether we process data concerning you and, if so, to receive a copy of it together with the information in this notice.
  • Right to rectification (Article 16) — to have inaccurate data corrected and incomplete data completed.
  • Right to erasure, "the right to be forgotten" (Article 17) — to have your data deleted where one of the grounds in that Article applies. This right is not absolute. Three limits apply, and we will tell you which one is engaged: (a) accounting records must be kept for 8 years — a legal obligation under the Accounting Act (Article 17(3)(b)); (b) contract data is kept to the end of the 5-year limitation period for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims (Article 17(3)(e)); (c) the suppression record described in section 4.5 is kept so we do not contact you again by mistake. Everything else is erased.
  • Right to restriction of processing (Article 18) — to have processing limited to mere storage while, for example, a dispute about accuracy is resolved.
  • Right to data portability (Article 20) — where processing is based on consent or on a contract and is carried out by automated means, to receive your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format, and to have it transmitted to another controller.
  • Right to object (Article 21) — to object at any time to processing based on our legitimate interests. Where you object to direct marketing, we must stop immediately and without exception.
  • Right to withdraw consent (Article 7(3)) — at any time, as easily as it was given. This does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.
  • Right not to be subject to automated decision-making (Article 22) — we do not carry out any such decision-making or profiling.

11. Data security

We apply technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk, as required by GDPR Article 32, including:

  • encryption in transit (TLS) for all connections to our services;
  • access on a need-to-know basis, with individual accounts and no shared credentials;
  • regular, tested backups held for BACKUP_ROTATION_PERIOD on a rolling cycle; data erased from live systems disappears from backups within that period, and backups are used only for restoration, never searched;
  • separation of client environments from one another;
  • prompt application of security updates to operating systems and applications;
  • logging of administrative access.

In the event of a personal data breach likely to result in a risk to the rights and freedoms of natural persons, we notify the supervisory authority within 72 hours (Article 33) and, where the risk is high, inform the affected data subjects without undue delay (Article 34). Where we act as a processor, we notify the client controller without undue delay so that they can meet that deadline.
